Sustaining Rate Simulator

Each unit generates a one-time payout of energy on acquisition. Some of it is banked as score, some paid out as commission to the recruiter, and the rest funds upkeep for every active unit. This runs the day-by-day minimum recruitment needed to stay afloat.
